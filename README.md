### 🔬 Research & Publications

My applied research focuses on organizational engineering — designing systems that make hybrid and remote teams measurably healthier and more effective.

| Publication | DOI / Link | Keywords |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 📄 **Productivity and Psychological Safety in Hybrid Teams: Designing a Management Framework** *(Bgust Model — Preprint, Zenodo, 2026)* | [![DOI](https://img.shields.io/badge/DOI-10.5281%2Fzenodo.19304388-blue?style=flat-square)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19304388) | Hybrid Work · Psychological Safety · Agile Governance · Value Metrics |
| 📄 **Additional preprints and working papers** | [![ORCID](https://img.shields.io/badge/ORCID-0009--0007--2432--1190-A6CE39?style=flat-square&logo=orcid&logoColor=white)](https://orcid.org/0009-0007-2432-1190) | Organizational Engineering · Data-Driven Leadership |

**About the Bgust Model:** A three-pillar organizational operating system designed to resolve the "leader's blind spot" in hybrid work environments: (1) **Agile/Lean Program Governance** to optimize value flow; (2) **Value Metrics Architecture** to replace vanity metrics with outcome indicators; and (3) **Human Development Systems** to institutionalize psychological safety. Validated on a field study of n=52 — 76.9% of the team showed "productivity theater" patterns and psychological safety at risk (M=3.12/5).
